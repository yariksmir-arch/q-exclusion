# Quantitative Comparison Model Zoo

All models are finite end-to-end L1 presentations.  Raw labels are syntax;
`<=` denotes only the stated declared interpretation of a descended output.

| ID | finite witness and result | boundary certified |
|---|---|---|
| QC-M1 | `a0~a1=A`, `b0~b1=B`; every raw pair reports the class-order label. | Pair saturation and fibre constancy give a unique relation. |
| QC-M2 | Same quotient; `(a0,b0)` reports `left`, `(a1,b0)` reports `tie`. | `COMPARISON NOT DEFINED`; no representative may be selected. |
| QC-M3 | QC-M2 plus declared probe separating `a0,a1`. | Concrete available refinement repairs descent. |
| QC-M4 | QC-M2 with only the original constant-on-`{a0,a1}` probe grammar. | Mathematical split exists but `COMPARISON REFINEMENT NOT REALIZED`. |
| QC-M5 | On `{a,b,c}`, relation has diagonal plus `a<=b`, `b<=c`, not `a<=c`. | Descended comparison is not automatically transitive; closure adds content. |
| QC-M6 | Distinct classes `a,b` with every pair comparable. | Preorder, not partial order; mutual comparison is not empirical identity. |
| QC-M7 | `{0,p,q}` with `0<=p,0<=q`, diagonal, and `p,q` incomparable. | Partial order does not imply total order. |

| QC-M14 | Same partial-sum domain: equal typed right sums imply equal addends. | Positive finite/partial cancellation control; not ordinary-track input. |
| QC-M15 | Deliberately stronger externally specified ordered group-like signature. | Only a theorem-specific future premise audit could address it; no admission here. |

## Adversarial claim disposition

1. Raw comparison automatically descends — refuted by QC-M2.
2. Descended comparison is transitive — refuted by QC-M5.
3. Transitive closure is scientist-level — refuted: it adds `(a,c)` in QC-M5.
4. Mutual comparison is empirical identity — refuted by QC-M6/M17.
5. Partial order is total — refuted by QC-M7.
6. Order determines addition — refuted by QC-M10.
7. Descended composition is monotone — refuted by QC-M8.
8. Monotonicity implies cancellation — refuted by QC-M9.
9. Ordered cancellative structure automatically yields a real scale — not
   established; theorem-specific regularity premises remain absent.
10. Numerical representation makes values L2 facts — refuted by QC-M16/RD.
11. Finite rational record arithmetic supplies magnitude — not typed: it is
    distinct record multiplicity arithmetic.
12. Raw numeric labels have quantitative meaning — refuted by QC-M12.

The executable harness covers QC-M1–M10, M12–M14, M16, and M17. M11/M15 are
documentary interface and admission controls; finite fixtures are witnesses,
not proofs of the general theorems.
