# Interface questions and experiential compression

# Interface comparison — known machinery, scoped application

## DEF IQ-D3: finite interface and bridge

For a named finite diagnostic comparison carrier X, a deterministic interface
is a total record map r:X→I. Its supported partition is ker r. X is L0
comparison data, not a world-state or a source of all interfaces. A represented
L1 procedure may supply r; otherwise it remains diagnostic. I is im r for
factor/minimality claims. Interface record, available procedures, and empirical
quotient are not identical objects. No consciousness predicate is introduced.

A bridge F:I→J, with s=Fr, is L0 diagnostic or a declared L1 procedure subject
to empirical descent before L2 admission. It is supplied, not canonically
inverted. Network edges may be absent, partial or relational; the propositions
below assert only the explicitly total deterministic case. There is no universal
ordered ladder, joint X for every interface, or global network completion.

## PROP IQ1: residual composition across a lossy bridge — KNOWN_EQUIVALENT

Assume finite named R,I,J,A, total U:R→I, Q:R→A and F:I→J.
For every j:

`Ans_(Q,FU)(j) = union { Ans_(Q,U)(i) : F(i)=j }`.

Proof: FU(e)=j iff U(e)=i for some i with F(i)=j. Apply Q to this
union of fibres. Empty fibres contribute ∅. This is direct-image composition,
not a new information law, stochastic theorem or ontology.
If Q descends through U as q on im U, it descends through FU iff q is constant
on the occupied F-fibres. In particular q(x)≠q(y) and F(x)=F(y) obstruct a
factor through F. They do not imply x=y or a hidden variable.

Failure tests: an unrelated J-record need not obey the formula; Q must be
transported with the comparison family; a nonsaturated partial Q cannot be
treated as total. F=id can lose nothing; constant Q cannot witness loss even
when F is lossy. Surjectivity is unnecessary. Finiteness is for fixtures, not
essential to this set-image identity. No probability assumption is used.
Q-audit: the obstruction is exactly ker(FU)⊄ker Q. Bijection transport of all
four carriers preserves the formula. This result applies literally to both
stress-tests, with the same R,U,Q,F types; no experiential/quantum analogy is
needed. It establishes neither a cross-domain explanatory theory nor novelty.

## PROP IQ2: sufficient records and constrained minima — KNOWN_EQUIVALENT

On a fixed finite X, v is at least as informative as r iff ker v⊆ker r;
equivalently r=fv on im v. Proof: assign the common r-value to each v-cell.
This is ordinary partition/factorization order. All arbitrary sufficient
refinements of r for Q have diagnostic least refinement (r,Q) (QD3).

Available repairs instead range over an independently specified procedure
menu P, with v≥r and ker v⊆ker Q. Minimal means no strictly coarser sufficient
member *inside P*. There need not be a least member or a unique minimum.
CM: X={0,1,2,3}, r constant, Q=(0,0,1,1), and menu {r,v,w} where
v=(0,0,1,2), w=(0,1,2,2). Both suffice; their kernels are incomparable;
r fails. They are both minimal in this menu, while (r,Q) is not in the menu.
If all probes are added, the claimed absence of a diagnostic minimum becomes
false. A mathematical table or kernel certificate alone does not implement P.

## Statistical experiments: distinct optional input

Declare Θ, outcome alphabets and probability kernels P_i(·|θ) independently.
A stochastic garbling K with P_J=K P_I gives Blackwell comparison. Finite
Le Cam directional deficiency uses `inf_K sup_θ TV(K P_I(·|θ),P_J(·|θ))`;
direction, normalization and allowed K must be declared. This section is a
comparison, not an imported probability axiom or a new proved theorem.
Deterministic partition loss is its noiseless special case; randomization and
decision loss require extra inputs. TV approximation does not certify exact
Q-factorization or availability. Categorical coherence is not a stochastic
deficiency test. See [bibliography](../docs/public/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md).

Anti-ontology checkpoint: no minimal relation is used to assemble a world.
Binary encodings can preserve arbitrarily rich declared signatures; fewer
symbols alone say nothing about weaker empirical content or ontic priority.

# Consciousness: separate interface questions, no phenomenal primitive

Scope: L0 finite conditional models of **declared distinctions**. E,M,L below
are interface alphabets, not ontological strata or bearers of consciousness.
The interpretation of E as experiential discrimination is externally stipulated;
this package neither derives nor verifies that interpretation from reports.
No primitive flag, observer or consciousness predicate is added to L1/L2.
An actual experiential interface needs independent evidence of its distinctions;
without that evidence it is `CONTRAST_NOT_AVAILABLE`, not a behavioural definition.

## CM IQ-C10: finite compression

E={a,b,c,d}; m:E→M maps a,b↦0 and c,d↦1; l:M→L maps both values to `r`.
The complete E distinction is not report-recoverable. m(a)=m(b) does not imply
a=b; l(0)=l(1) does not imply equal memory distinctions. Letters name
declared contrast values only, not absolute identities through time.

Question q:E→{warm,cool} takes (warm,warm,cool,cool). At M it has a factor,
at L its residual is {warm,cool}. The finer q'=id_E already fails at M.
Apply IQ1 with R=E, U=m, F=l, Q=q: two singleton memory residuals merge
into the two-answer report residual. This is exactly the same theorem and
typing used in the quantum rank example, not a metaphor about superposition.
Independent extra readout k:E→{0,1}=(0,1,0,1) together with m separates E
mathematically. Its availability must be independently established; writing k
does not provide an attention/memory procedure.

Same report can coexist with different richer structures: two two-entry
extension packages have constant report `r`, one with a single E contrast
and one with two. Their richer contrast cardinality differs. This comparison
is diagnostic and does not smuggle a phenomenal flag into the core. Choosing
only packages with one E contrast makes recovery trivial by class restriction,
not by report evidence (`COMPARISON_CLASS_DEPENDENT`).

Semantic re-description preserves a question only under independently declared
bijection of its answer contrast and commuting bridges. Calling a neural/report
label an experience without such a bridge changes the target or encodes it.
After compression the finer question is unsupported **at L**, not meaningless
at E or in every enriched theory. No hard-problem solution, substrate-independence
claim, reduction to behaviour or phenomenal primacy is established.

Anti-ontology checkpoint: the E→M→L diagram is one conditional compression
example. It is not a universal ladder, causal history, origin of experience,
or construction of a world from elementary differences.
