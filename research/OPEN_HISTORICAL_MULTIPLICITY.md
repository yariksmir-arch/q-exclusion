# Stream Report — Open historical multiplicity

## 1. Objective

Clarify what adopting Structural Sufficiency (SS) licenses about historical organization, without adding worlds, selectors, a global perspective, a universal history domain or fundamental time. This is an optional conceptual audit using existing mathematics. The public explanation is [Open histories](../docs/public/OPEN_HISTORIES.md).

## 2. Starting normative assumptions

Frozen Core §§1–2 and 9 distinguish declared content from representation and added facts; §§3–8 govern concrete repertoires, empirical descent and refinement. Formal Language A–I governs typing and modal/time vocabulary. Research Governance requires explicit assumptions and failure models. Philosophical SS, as explained in [the baseline](../docs/public/OVERVIEW.md), is a separate commitment, not proved by factorization.

No new core assumption is proposed. Each conditional construction below uses a named finite or otherwise concretely supplied comparison domain. Its existence is an input; SS does not supply it. Cardinalities refer only to that domain or its quotient. No numerical tolerance, preferred boundary, common clock or human articulation is assumed.

## 3. Definitions introduced

**DEF OH-D1 — historical organization (interpretive role).** A concrete arrangement of discriminations, relations, records and represented dependencies may be examined as a historical organization when its record and dependence claims have been independently specified. This is a non-core interpretive role at L0/L1, not a new sort with an existence axiom. A list, graph or trace is a representation; its index order is not automatically physical time or causation. A causal reading additionally requires a supplied intervention/process semantics. The word historical alone licenses nothing.

**DEF OH-D2 — comparison interface.** For one declared sector, let H be a chosen L0 carrier representing the arrangements under comparison, R a concrete family of compatible represented tests, and r_i:H→V_i their total readouts (i in the declared R). Value carriers V_i are chosen representational labels. For partial tests, availability must be recorded as well; these results do not identify disabled tests with empty output. Define h ≈ k iff every r_i agrees. The quotient E=H/≈ and q:H→E are derived diagnostics; they are L2 only when these are the declared contextual tests, or descent to the existing empirical quotient is separately proved. Dependence: Frozen Core §§3–8. No H of all histories is introduced. Quotient uniqueness is up to the specified transport, not a canonical raw representative.

**DEF OH-D3 — retention interface.** m:H→M is a supplied record map; a:D⊆H→H is a supplied partial process. H,M,D,m,a are optional L1 data represented at L0, not inferred from SS. Their L2 use requires their own domain/value descent. A record is sufficient for R when ker m⊆≈. An exact update on record values requires D to be ker m-saturated and m(a(h)) constant on each defined m-fibre. A preservation claim for a particular contrast r means the existence of a decoder d on im(m∘a|D) with r|D=d∘m∘a|D. All decoders here are mathematical factors until implementation is supplied. This definition adds no identity through time.

**DEF OH-D4 — scoped questions about individuality.** Distinguishability is inequality in E. Retained distinguishability is certified only for the specified record/process interface and target. Articulation is represented availability of discriminations, records and comparisons; neither language nor a person is required. Stability means preservation under a named process family, not longevity above an arbitrary threshold. Independent-channel agreement means declared non-importation certificates and a usable comparison interface as in [ICC2–5](INDEPENDENT_CONSTRAINT_CONVERGENCE.md); it is not absolute causal or statistical independence. These are separate questions about a supplied organization and task, not a universal recognition criterion for historical individuality, singly or jointly. SS as interpreted here supplies no universal individuality checklist. Their failure can remove public checking or retention without proving that an organization does not exist. No absolute boundary or unique maximal individual is defined.

## 4. Results

**HEUR OH-H1 — open multiplicity under SS.** SS does not establish absolute historical uniqueness: the claim that there is only one history requires structural grounds of its own. A privileged history, distinguished from others by a criterion or selector, is a separate claim. SS rules out an independently varying historical selector with no organizational difference. It supplies neither absolute historical uniqueness nor an objective collection of worlds. Multiple substantively different organizations are permitted when their relations license the differences. Permission is not an existence theorem for many worlds. Open means no completion axiom is introduced; it does not mean provably infinite, necessarily growing or obligatorily branching.

The burden is asymmetric between an extra uniqueness assertion and withholding that assertion: the former needs grounds; the latter need not establish many worlds or branch probabilities. The burden is symmetric between positive claims of one and many: both require articulated content. A structurally justified uniqueness result on a specified domain is allowed. Equal local reports do not establish exhaustive organizational equality; SS cannot be applied as if an impoverished local signature were complete.

**PROP OH-P1 — determination does not select a history.** Assume OH-D2 and a declared non-core diagnostic function F:H→Y, where Y is a chosen L0 value carrier with specified value semantics. F factors uniquely through q on E iff ≈⊆ker F. That factor can take many different values. Conversely, a singleton E says only that the declared tests do not distinguish H. It does not imply a singleton H, one causal account, or one world.

**PROP OH-P2 — joint checking and retention have independent obligations.** Assume OH-D2–3. A record m determines every r_i iff ker m⊆⋂_i ker r_i. An exact partial record update exists iff domain saturation and output constancy in OH-D3 hold. Even their conjunction does not imply that a process preserves distinctions: preservation of r after a requires ker(m∘a|D)⊆ker(r|D). Each clause is independently testable. Channel provenance is not needed for the algebraic intersection; it is extra evidence needed for the stronger claim of independent confirmation.

**NO-GO OH-N1 — records do not fix a causal reconstruction.** On a declared two-element diagnostic extension domain {e_direct,e_common}, suppose U forgets whether two equal terminal records were generated by a direct channel or by a common input. Let F label these two formation graphs. Then U(e_direct)=U(e_common), while F differs. F does not descend. This is an explicit failure for this record-only reduct, not an impossibility of learning causation with richer interventions. Replacing the forgotten graph by a hidden unique history is NEW THEORY REQUIRED relative to that reduct. Neither extension is asserted to be a world.

**NO-GO OH-N2 — no representation-independent raw history count.** A two-element carrier with constant tests and a one-element carrier with the same profile image have isomorphic empirical signatures when only those readouts are declared. Raw cardinalities differ. A count of worlds cannot be obtained by counting representatives. If a descended multiplicity-sensitive operation is added, this example no longer preserves that enriched signature; that is additional content, not a repair by relabelling.

**OPEN OH-O1.** Obtain a concrete nontrivial historical target for which implemented record retention, independent formation and intervention constraints determine more than an existing record/control/ICC result. No such new theorem or physical prediction is established here.

**CONJ OH-C1 (research hypothesis, not admission rule).** In a specified empirical application, adding independently formed, jointly comparable and retained records may make a target historical distinction more robust under declared process perturbations. Testing requires the target, admissible perturbations and formation certificates before observing the outcome, plus copied-channel and erasure controls. It is not a universal claim: redundant copies can add no discrimination, and a common disturbance can erase every channel. No universal threshold or mandatory number of channels follows.

## 5. Proofs / proof sketches

OH-P1: a factor forces equal q-values to have equal F-values. Conversely assign to a class the common F-value of its elements; fibre constancy makes this a function without selecting representatives. Uniqueness follows from surjectivity of q. For the non-uniqueness clause take H={0,1}, r(h)=h, F=r. Both distinctions descend; no element is designated. For the singleton-quotient clause use constant r on that same H. These are logical models of the formal admission discipline, not empirical models proving philosophical SS.

OH-P2: apply P1 separately to r_i with forgetting map m onto its image. For updates, a descended domain must pull back exactly to D; this is saturation. Output constancy makes the rule m(h)↦m(a(h)) single-valued. Conversely the descended domain and rule imply both conditions. Apply P1 once more to m∘a|D for the preservation clause. These are existing image-factorization and partial-operation descent arguments, not new dynamics. A process can be deterministic on records while erasing every prior distinction.

OH-N1 is a two-point nonconstant fibre. Operationally, both a copying circuit and two outputs of a common input can yield terminal pair (v,v); the observation map discards the circuit. Their different responses to an independently implemented intervention can separate them, but intervention data were not present in the stated reduct. OH-N2 follows by sending each quotient class to its common constant profile; both quotients are singletons. An isomorphism here is a comparison of descriptions, not an ontic transformation.

A relation graph with two incomparable nodes also admits different linearizations. Acyclicity alone therefore does not select a clock, a total order or causal semantics. Histories can split, merge or recur in an optional process model; an irreversible tree is not imposed.

## 7. Q-audit

Test candidate selectors, counts, boundaries and provenance facts by an explicit forgetful map. If they vary at fixed retained structure they fail descent; call this NEW THEORY REQUIRED, not an enriched theorem of the old theory. If they are just new names for existing records, they add no content. A quotient class is not an ontological world. Distinct empirical classes certify scoped differences; equal classes certify only the stated repertoire's inability to distinguish.

Semantic audit: individuality means a declared distinguishability/retention claim, not an invisible bearer; fixation means record retention for stated operations, not irreversible cosmic fact; articulation means an implemented relation/readout, not human naming; independence is scoped non-importation; history means a represented relational organization with independently justified semantics; openness means absence of a universal closure commitment. No probability of existence, preferred decomposition, absolute boundary, global family of perspectives or independently varying identity tag is licensed. SS is not an instruction to dismiss distinctions simply because a local test is absent.

## 8. Presentation-invariance audit

P1–P2 transport under bijections of the relevant carriers/readout values preserving the named tests, record maps and process graphs. Saturated domains must transport too. Profile-equivalent changing carriers preserve E, but do not automatically transport a raw process or a Q-extension fibre. N1 requires transport of both the extension and its forgetful map; weak empirical signature agreement alone is insufficient. No canonical representative is selected. Refinement claims require unchanged meanings of old tests and compatible coarse maps. Forgetting a test may merge classes, so raw historical individuality is not asserted invariant under loss of evidence.

## 9. Overfitting and novelty audit

The mathematical results specialize existing record sufficiency (CRS1), partial update/control criteria (CMQ4/7), co-reference diagnostics (CI5–12), and independent constraint convergence (ICC2–9). Their motivation also applies to copied files, circuit traces and distributed records. No quantum, spacetime, observer or probability assumption enters the proofs. Removing the words history and individuality leaves exactly the same mathematics.

Verdict: KNOWN / PROJECT_SPECIFIC_RECOMBINATION; no certified theorem novelty. The useful project delta is the distinction between non-entailment of absolute uniqueness, positive scoped multiplicity and world ontology, together with the separate audits for discrimination, retention, provenance and causal reconstruction. A proposed universal individuality score, branch count or threshold is outside this interpretation of SS, rather than a missing result to pursue. The negative research result concerns additional formal content: this pass adds none beyond existing modules.

External scientific comparisons, primary references and their limits are given in [Open histories](../docs/public/OPEN_HISTORIES.md). These comparisons are interpretive, not identification or derivation of those physical theories. Source selection is targeted, not an exhaustive priority search.
