# Stream Report - Structural Sufficiency, modality and possible worlds

## 1. Objective

Develop a positive modal interpretation conditional on philosophical SS, consistent with Open Historical Multiplicity, without new ontological primitives or a universal modal language. Public exposition: [SS and modality](../docs/public/MODALITY.md). This report applies existing Q/descent, conditioning and countermodels; no new mathematical module or theorem is proposed.

## 2. Starting normative assumptions

Frozen Core sections 1-2: a declared presentation is not a global world object; Q is theory-relative factorization. Sections 3-8: concrete repertoires, empirical signatures, represented operations and scoped refinement. Section 9: mathematical representation adds no physical content. Formal Language A-D and F-I require levels, typing, concrete modal domains and independently supplied time/probability/recognition structure. Research Governance requires explicit assumptions, failures and audits.

Philosophical SS is the [baseline's](../docs/public/OVERVIEW.md) separate commitment against additional determinacy beyond substantive organization, not a consequence of the set-level Q theorem. Open Histories supplies no absolute historical uniqueness, total world domain or branch probability. None is introduced here.

## 3. Definitions introduced

No new core object. The following are local terminology and specializations of existing interfaces, not a universal semantics.

**DEF - scoped comparison data.** Use QD-D1/QD1 in [Question Definability](QUESTION_DEFINABILITY.md): a chosen finite L0 diagnostic extension carrier E, retained-content carrier B, independently specified answer carrier A, and total maps U:E->B and Q:E->A. All are non-core data, representing L1 content only where independently supplied. Choose one b in B and, for a conditional query, a supplied subset C of U^-1(b). Equality is the declared answer contrast; any alternative answer equivalence must be explicit. E is not all possibilities or histories. C is unique only after these inputs are fixed; it has no canonical ontology.

**DEF - scoped modal readings.** For C nonempty and a supplied predicate P:C->{0,1}, compatibility means existence of a witness with P=1; necessity relative to C means P=1 throughout C. These are derived L0 diagnostic verdicts, not axioms of physical or metaphysical modality. Empty C is reported as NO_COMPATIBLE_CASE, not certainty. A missing U, Q, predicate or comparison bridge is NOT_SPECIFIED, not an empty domain. Scientist-level promotion to L2 requires the relevant empirical domain/value descent and, for an asserted procedure, availability. These conventions depend on QD1 and the existing partial-domain discipline; no L3 structure is needed.

**DEF - articulation occurrence versus content.** Occurrence names a specified record-production or calculation process within declared L1 content; the corresponding L2 record exists only after its descent checks. Content is the independently typed target attribution and its semantic/evidential bridge. Both are non-core explanatory roles; neither creates an actuality predicate, person, truth-evaluator or global domain. A relation not represented by a current theory is not thereby absent. The philosophical phrase "an articulation occurs in relations" asserts no completed inventory of those relations.

**DEF - independently varying modal remainder (audit target).** This means a putative modal contrast stipulated to vary while substantive organization is held fixed. For a concrete formal audit only, use two diagnostic decorations e0,e1 over one supplied reduct b, with U(e0)=U(e1)=b and Q(e0)=0, Q(e1)=1. These are L0 test extensions, not admitted objects or possible worlds. Their Q-verdict is determined relative to U,Q; whether that reduct exhausts substantive organization is not a mathematical conclusion.

## 4. Results

**HEUR - strong conditional consequence of SS.** If SS is adopted, determinate modal content has no independently varying remainder beyond substantive differences and relations. A freestanding world catalogue cannot confer additional modal determinacy while that organization is held fixed. Modality is a derivative articulation of actual relations and constraints: compatibility, exclusion, capacities and dependence express substantive constraints of actuality under particular theoretical assumptions. Derivation here is explanatory, not necessarily temporal. SS requires no derivation from a wholly nonmodal basis; a demand for a separate ontological source of modality would reintroduce the modal primitive it rejects. This is stronger than "possible worlds have not been proved" and is not the weaker assertion that every modality is ignorance or fiction.

This is an ontological commitment conditional on SS, not a proof that a current model is adequate. It does not require a complete articulation even in principle, a common language for every contrast, human availability, eventual discovery, or a global truth-evaluator. Actual substantive differences can exceed a current account without becoming additional facts beyond substantive organization. A rival theory using world vocabulary for additional substantive relations is not refuted by a word ban; its new content needs its own grounds. This project adopts no global possible-world domain.

**Existing mathematics applied, not a new PROP.** QD1 gives Q[U^-1(b)] as the exact diagnostic answer image and point descent iff Q is constant on occupied U-fibres. Conditioning restricts the declared comparison class and its answer image. Compatibility witnesses, conditional necessities, ambiguity and inconsistency can therefore be stated precisely in a particular theory. A modal target varying in a U-fibre is NEW THEORY REQUIRED relative to that reduct. A philosopher must separately argue whether it is an inadmissible remainder under SS or a substantive difference absent from that account.

**HEUR - conditioning is not intervention.** Restriction by a condition answers a compatibility question. A causal counterfactual additionally requires a supplied process/intervention semantics, including what is changed and preserved. Neither SS nor a static compatibility table supplies that interface, a nearest-world ranking or a truth oracle. The [control](CONTROL_THEORY.md) and [interface](interfaces/SPECIFICATION.md) modules already separate readouts from supported changes.

**OPEN.** Adequacy of particular models and the warrant for their constraints, capacities, variation/intervention families and counterfactual dependencies remain matters for substantive investigation. These are obligations of particular explanations, not an unresolved demand for an independent source of modality or a wholly nonmodal basis. Under adopted SS, modality has no separate ontological remainder; no particular articulation is thereby complete.

## 5. Proofs / proof sketches

No new theorem. Apply QD1: if a factor Qbar on im U exists, equal retained content has equal answers. Conversely fibre constancy defines a class answer without choosing a representative. This proves determination relative to U, not SS. The two decorated extensions in section 3 fail this test immediately. Under SS, the additional philosophical premise is that substantive organization exhausts determinacy; only then is an independently varying modal remainder excluded. An incomplete B does not satisfy that premise merely by being called structure.

For concrete content take E={00,11}, with coordinates representing two jointly usable readout roles and constraint x=y. On E, changing the supplied condition from x=0 to x=1 changes the forced y-answer from 0 to 1. Neither tuple is selected as a world. Remove the constraint and take E={00,01,10,11}: x=0 no longer determines y. This proves the conditional inference depends on the supplied relation. Replacing it by x!=y yields the opposite conditional. Shared marginal alphabets do not determine the joint dependency.

An implemented change of x is a different input from conditioning. A model with constraints y=x allows y=0 under x=0. Another operation semantics can overwrite a separately constrained x while retaining y=1. A causal comparison therefore needs explicit update/replacement rules; literal conjunction with the old x=1 constraint instead gives inconsistency. None of these readings is licensed by the word "if" alone.

## 6. Countermodels and failed routes

| Kill-test | Explicit witness | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Token occurrence entails truth of content | E={(t,0),(t,1)}, U(t,v)=t, Q(t,v)=v | The same produced token accompanies different described values; content does not descend from token alone. |
| Mathematical permissibility entails realizable capacity | A supplied table permits 0->1, but a separately supplied implementation graph has only 0->0 | Compatibility is not evidence of implementation; changing the graph adds substantive content. |
| Equal observations entail equal modalities | Two devices have idle readout 0; under a supplied input one yields 1 and the other 0 | Their response graphs differ; the idle reduct was incomplete, not all organization held fixed. |
| Current nonseparation proves no difference | H={00,01}; only first-bit readout | Second bits differ in the richer specified presentation; current quotient agreement is scoped. |
| Every difference must eventually be articulated | Same H and constant generated first-bit repertoire retained under identity updates | No represented procedure ever separates the cases in this module; no general discovery promise follows. This is not proof of principled unarticulability. |
| Open histories means all alternatives coexist | E={00,11} used as a diagnostic comparison class | Enumeration supplies no instantiation map or coexistence claim. |
| Pairwise compatibility supplies a total world | Binary x=y, y=z, z!=x | Every pair of constraints is satisfiable but their joint class is empty. No global completion follows. |
| Local necessity is absolute necessity | C={0} versus expanded C'={0,1}; P(v)=1 iff v=0 | The earlier necessity changes under expansion; no absolute modal verdict was established. |
| Necessity follows from an empty class | C empty | Ordinary universal quantification is vacuous; the diagnostic returns inconsistency, not a substantive necessity. |
| Marginals determine counterfactual dependence | E_equal={00,11}, E_opposite={01,10} | Same marginal alphabets, opposed conditional outputs; a joint/intervention interface is missing. |
| A selector can repair counterfactual ambiguity | One coarse record with symmetric candidates 0 and 1 | Selecting either is not invariant under their swap without extra content; reuse QD4. |
| Possible-worlds semantics entails Lewisian ontology | The same finite semantic calculation receives either a formal-use or an ontological-reading tag | The calculation alone does not select the interpretation; this does not refute a separately argued ontology. |
| Independent modal tag follows from SS's mathematics | Decorations in section 3 | Q rejects derivability from the reduct; exclusion under SS is a separate philosophical commitment. |
| Zombie exclusion follows from equal reports | Constant report with richer experiential contrast left unspecified | A report quotient does not hold substantive organization fixed; the full zombie commitment cannot be inferred from it. |

These are failure cases for the respective inference steps, not new universal countermodels to metaphysical systems. None adds an assumption solely to obtain a physical target.

## 7. Q-audit

Semantic audit: distinguish (i) a mathematical witness, (ii) warranted capacity or dependence of the described arrangement, (iii) what a current articulation distinguishes, and (iv) SS's rejection of an independent remainder. These are not interchangeable.

The occurrence/content example explicitly forgets the described value. A representation can have a total valuation function without supplying a physical evaluator or warrant for every valuation. Its L0 evaluator is no more ontic than a graph coordinate. Descent of an attribution still needs its stated domains, target meanings and semantic bridge. No interpretation tag promotes a table to empirical adequacy or lawhood.

The positive interpretation does not quietly restrict substantive content to current tests. Under SS, an unarticulated difference can be a substantive relational difference. Q does not identify incomparable contents, deny phenomenal presence on missing evidence, or prove that a future repertoire exists. A full zombie is excluded because SS rejects phenomenal variation at fixed substantive organization, not because its absence can be read from behavioural data. Critics may reject that commitment; the modal interpretation does not decide their conceivability-to-possibility argument.

## 8. Presentation-invariance audit

Reuse QD6 and existing [presentation transport](PRESENTATION_TRANSPORT.md). Bijections of E,B,A preserving U,Q transport answer images. Predicates and conditioning subsets must be transported as well. For a semantic evaluator to descend, it must respect the chosen input/output equivalences; no preferred valuation or representative is inferred. Changing an intervention graph, condition meaning or answer contrast is new content, not mere recoding. Changing-carrier or categorical comparisons need their existing additional interface/coherence hypotheses.

A coarse quotient can erase a dependency or verifier distinction. Its successful Q-test is no certificate of exhaustive content. No family of all presentations or an inverse limit over all articulations is postulated.

## 9. Overfitting and novelty audit

Mathematics: KNOWN / EXISTING_APPARATUS. Reuse QD1/QD4/QD6, ordinary existential/universal quantification and constraint restriction, and existing control/coordination witnesses. No new theorem, formal module, axiom, physical prediction or universal modal semantics. Removing the word modality leaves those mathematical results intact.

Philosophical delta for this project: an explicit positive SS reading of modal determinacy, separating strong ontological non-independence from incompleteness of any current account; articulation occurrence from described content; and scoped conditional inference from causal counterfactuals. This clarifies an implication of an adopted commitment. It does not establish priority over the extensive literature below.

## 10. Dependency impact

Frozen typing/descent + existing question/control/constraint modules -> local diagnostics; philosophical SS -> conditional interpretation. ## 11. Regression risks

Risks: equating represented models with instantiations; treating necessity in a supplied class as unrestricted necessity; importing interventions from static restriction; treating an L0 valuation as a privileged evaluator; confusing incomplete access with absence; defining organization as whatever secures the modal answer. The genuine circularity risk is defining "substantive organization" solely through the modal conclusions it is then invoked to justify. Response relations, conditions and target contrasts need grounds beyond restating those conclusions; these grounds need not be wholly nonmodal. For example, merely renaming "the device can output 1" as its organization explains nothing; a specified construction, coupling and supported response relation supplies content whose adequacy can be challenged. This is a schematic explanatory contrast, not a new theorem or universal adequacy criterion. The remaining objection concerns the warrant for particular attributions, not a separate origin of modality. It leaves SS's conditional exclusion of an independent modal remainder intact.
