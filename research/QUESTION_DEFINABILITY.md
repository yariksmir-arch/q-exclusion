# Question definability / repair — optional audit interface

Status: standard identification with contrast audit. The corrected RD6 value-contrast criterion is included below. This is a checklist over existing mathematics.

## Types and independent inputs (DEF QD-D1)

Declare finite sets E, B, A and total maps U:E→B, Q:E→A. E is a specified
extension/realization comparison class, never all worlds; A is an independently
specified answer contrast, not an inferred philosophical interpretation.
These are L0 diagnostics representing declared L1 interfaces where supplied;
their empirical promotion is a separate L2 descent test. No L3 representation
is admitted by this construction. All data, repertoire, target equality and
comparison class are inputs, unique only relative to their declaration and
adequate transport. U need not be surjective. Empty E is permitted; A may
be empty only when a total Q exists. Absence of a typed Q is a specification
failure, not an empty answer set. Answer equivalence, if used, is explicit
and replaces A by its declared quotient.

## Residual answers (PROP QD1 — standard identification)

Ans_Q(b)=Q[U⁻¹(b)] defines B→P(A). It is the unique least envelope under
pointwise inclusion satisfying Q(e)∈Ans_Q(Ue). Proof: every envelope must
contain each displayed Q(e), and their image itself satisfies the condition.
Q descends to a unique point map on im U iff every occupied fibre image is
singleton, equivalently ker U⊆ker Q (T0.4/AI1). On unused B labels an arbitrary
point extension is neither unique nor supplied; if A=∅ and B≠∅ none exists.
Thus singleton means point identification on the tested fibre, multiple means
non-point identification, empty means no compatible declared extension.
An inconsistent class does not prove a point answer by vacuous constancy.

The residual is exact relative to these inputs, not necessarily the best
empirically executable output. A map producing it must pass its own empirical
descent and available decoder/procedure tests (RR/CRS/AI7). Multiple answers
do not supply a selected actuality or one hidden unknown answer. Such additional
data may exist in an enriched theory; Q failure does not imply nonexistence.

## Dual repair (PROP QD2/QD3 — standard universal properties)

On im Q generate an equivalence ≈ from all pairs Q(e),Q(e') with Ue=Ue'.
Take its reflexive/symmetric/transitive closure. Then c:im Q→im Q/≈ makes
cQ descend, where Q is explicitly corestricted to im Q. If d:im Q→D makes dQ descend, d is constant on the generators,
hence on ≈, so d=dbar c uniquely. This is the finest sufficient answer
coarsening, up to quotient isomorphism. No answer representative is selected.
An extension of c over unused A labels needs a convention; the universal
property tested here is on im Q. Transitive chains through different fibres
can collapse every answer: the canonical quotient need not be useful.
Coarsening changes the question and does not establish an original point answer.

The joint diagnostic refinement r(e)=(Ue,Qe) has kernel ker U∩ker Q.
For every v with ker v⊆ker U and ker v⊆ker Q, r factors uniquely through
im v. Thus r is coarsest base refinement preserving U and determining Q.
This is CRS5/AI1; T0.5 adds operational stability only for independently
declared operations K via C_K(ker U∩ker Q). Neither gives available probes,
execution, recursive updates or a non-circular empirical boundary. Appending
Q as an available readout without independent availability is precisely the
forbidden conclusion-as-probe move.

## Restriction repair (NO-GO QD4)

For declared E'⊆E, Ans_(Q|E')(b)⊆Ans_Q(b). On E={0,1}, U constant,
Q=id, the two maximal nonempty resolving restrictions {0},{1} are exchanged
by the automorphism swapping extensions and answers. No equivariant selection
of one restriction exists. Maximal restrictions exist finitely but no general
unique/canonical winner follows. Invariance of the whole selected family is
allowed; preserving every old base fibre is an additional assumption. A
restriction can empty a fibre rather than answer it. Strengthened assumptions
are new input, not a proof about the original class.

## Whole-query firewall (NO-GO QD5)

On one two-element fibre, F=G=id each fails point descent, but F XOR G=0
descends. Ans_(F,G)={(0,0),(1,1)} is strictly smaller than
Ans_F×Ans_G. The identity Ans_h(F,G)=h[Ans_(F,G)] uses the joint image,
not a product substitution. Constant change with ambiguous persistence is
a zero-change contrast can coexist with ambiguous persistence; CI joint completions, CPG-M2 and CPC4 supply finite witnesses.
Gauge-like parity of two simultaneously flipped Boolean coordinates is an
L0 analogy only, not a physical symmetry process. A descended composite does
not make internal variables available. Partial questions need domain saturation
plus output constancy (T0.2); a declared undefined tag changes the answer
contrast and must be transported. Preserve empty cases and joint domains.

## Anti-vacuity and transport (NO-GO QD6; existing T1/CC)

Choosing U=q_(ker Q) forces every Q to descend. Choosing constant U blocks
every nonconstant Q. This is useful as a mathematical diagnostic minimum,
but vacuous as an independent justification of the boundary. U, repertoire,
extension class and contrast require external structural/procedural justification
before admitting a target as old-theory content. Question definability neither
chooses the boundary nor establishes verificationism/Structural Sufficiency.

Adequate bijections α:E→E', β:B→B', γ:A→A' with U'α=βU and Q'α=γQ
give Ans_Q'(βb)=γ[Ans_Q(b)] and preserve all stated statuses. General
transport reuses T1/CC/RD and needs their hypotheses; bare relabeling or a
nonbijective answer map need not preserve ambiguity. Refinement U=tV gives
Q[V⁻¹(v)]⊆Q[U⁻¹(tv)]; narrowing shrinks and expansion can enlarge answers.
Nothing implies a final repertoire/refinement. Groupoid RD3b retains the
independent morphism-coherence obstruction; singleton object images alone
are not coherent categorical descent.

## Recognition contrast erratum (CM QD-RD6)

RD5's Val target retains (C,j,v). Nonisomorphism of these triples can result
solely from C. It does not imply disagreement of the evaluated value in a
fixed answer contrast. Let S={a,b} have endpoint operations only. Two total
rational barycentric meet algebras on chains a<b and a<b<c preserve S.
For strict weights their operation is meet; endpoints select the specified
input. Their pointed evaluation triples are nonisomorphic by size, yet
mix(a,b)=j(a) in both. More decisively, mix(a,a)=j(a) in every total
barycentric completion by idempotence, while the two ambient triples remain
nonisomorphic. Omitting a procedure from S does not supply its execution;
an algebraically identified answer and an available mixing procedure differ.

The repaired RD6 is RD4 applied to the specified value contrast, with the
explicit implication that a reduct-defined queried answer would give a
factor of that contrast. RD7 still witnesses named-output disagreement
j(a) versus j(b). The old criterion remains valid for the entire decorated
evaluation datum; its inference to an arbitrary missing-value question was
too strong. The value-contrast statement below, rather than ambient non-isomorphism, governs this result. Frozen core and recognition admission are not upgraded.

## Classification and use

QD1–6 are standard image/quotient/identification arguments, existing T0/CRS
specializations or elementary countermodels. The workflow
`reduct → contrast → whole-query descent → residual → availability → repair`
is an optional admission checklist with no established external novelty.
It covers deterministic exhaustive-answer/partition questions only, not every
linguistic question: overlapping resolving answers (supports {0,1} and {1,2})
need not be cells of a unique-answer partition. Set-theoretic factorization
does not imply formula definability in a chosen language (Beth's converse has
additional logical hypotheses), statistical inference or general semantics.
It retains identified sets, weakens questions, diagnoses interface enrichment
or records stronger assumptions without selecting one remedy. No general
claim of cognitive, metaphysical, truth/reference or physical reconstruction
follows. See [scope](../docs/public/SCOPE.md), [bibliography](../docs/public/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md) and [verification](../docs/public/REPRODUCIBILITY.md).
