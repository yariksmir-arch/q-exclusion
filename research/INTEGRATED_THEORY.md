# Quotients, descent and available refinement

## Core proofs

This is the proof source for T0.1–T0.6 and the realized-refinement application RR4–7. Identifiers name existing scoped applications of known mathematics.

**Declared data (DEF, derived/diagnostic L0 representations of L1; quotient parts L2 only after descent).** Supply typed carriers A_s, a stated family of finite-arity partial operations f:D_f→A_t and Boolean relations, and sort-indexed functional totalized test observations p:A_s→O_p. A failure label records only undefinedness of that declared test. The repertoire closure contains exactly represented plug-ins/compositions. All carriers, domains, alphabets, composition rules and target equalities are inputs, chosen up to adequate transport. Equivalences, quotient classes and factors are unique relative to those inputs, not privileged encodings.

**T0.1/T0.3 — kernel and universal property.** Assume those functional observations and a declared contextual closure. Define theta by equality of every observation. Equality is reflexive, symmetric and transitive for each observation; intersection preserves these properties, so theta is an equivalence. Every p factors through q:A→A/theta by pbar([a])=p(a). Conversely if a quotient v:A→V makes every p factor, v(a)=v(b) implies every p(a)=p(b), hence theta(a,b). Thus ker v⊆theta and q factors uniquely through im v. The empty test family gives a one-class quotient on each nonempty sort, not knowledge of absent targets. A source bit pair with only the first-bit probe cannot supply the second bit.

**T0.2/T0.4 — operations, domains and relations.** For a proposed operation on q-classes, assume D_f is saturated under the product input equivalence and q_t f is constant on each enabled product fibre. Define its quotient domain as q(D_f) and its value by [f(a)]. Saturation makes enabledness representative-independent; output constancy makes the value independent. Surjectivity onto quotient classes proves uniqueness. Conversely a commuting quotient operation determines both a domain flag and an output class for every input class, so both conditions are necessary. A Boolean relation descends exactly when membership is product-fibre invariant. For total operations domain saturation is automatic. This is strong representative-independent descent, not an existentially enabled relation semantics.

Countermodels: on {a,b,c} with a theta b but c separate, f(a)=a,f(b)=c fails output constancy; a partial f enabled at a but not b fails saturated-domain descent even if all enabled outputs agree. If a represented plug-in distinguishes a and b, omitting it from the claimed complete closure invalidates a congruence claim. No completion over unrepresented plug-ins is supplied.

**T0.5 — diagnostic stable repair.** For the supplied finite-arity operation/relation family K and a typed equivalence theta, consider K-stable equivalences alpha⊆theta: each domain and Boolean relation is alpha-saturated and every operation preserves alpha on its domain. Their join is generated by finite chains. Each chain step preserves domain/relation flags. Replacing finitely many input coordinates one step at a time gives output pairs linked by these stable equivalences; transitivity relates the endpoints. Hence the join is K-stable and lies below theta. It contains every candidate, so it is the unique greatest stable subequivalence C_K(theta), giving the coarsest stable quotient refinement. Identity is a candidate. Infinitary arities and non-Boolean relation semantics require separate hypotheses; they are not covered by the finite-coordinate proof.

This repair is a partition, not a new test. With r(a)=r(b)=0,r(c)=1 and f(a)=a,f(b)=c, the stable repair splits a,b. If the available grammar has only r and no represented r∘f or other separator, that partition cannot be implemented. RR7 is the correct outcome.

**T0.6 and RR4–7 — represented refinement and realization.** Assume theta_R''⊆theta_R'⊆theta_R for a stated chain of repertoire extensions. The map [a]_R'↦[a]_R is well-defined, and successive maps compose because both paths send a fine class to its coarse class. First-bit and second-bit repertoires on four rows are incomparable, so no such map is generally supplied. For realization of a proposed alpha, soundness of every generated probe gives alpha⊆ker P; separation of each distinct alpha pair gives ker P⊆alpha. Hence equality. On a finite set of required pairs select one available grammar-generated separator per pair; soundness of the whole represented closure prevents over-splitting. No separator, unavailable routing or an unsound closure defeats the realization argument. This is existing RR/T0 mathematics.

**Transport and Q-audit.** An adequate presentation comparison must carry sorts, domains, functional observations, operation graphs, represented closure and equality contrasts. It then carries the foregoing kernel/chain arguments and quotient squares. Bare carrier isomorphism or quotient equality alone is insufficient. Q-status additionally requires the extension-fibre comparison of T1.2/RD3b; objectwise constancy alone does not prove categorical coherent descent. All proofs are textual mathematical arguments, not proof-assistant verification. They add no probability, global state, subject, time or privileged decomposition.
