## Exact sequential descent

**THM CP1 (partial-composition descent).** A unique L2 partial map `bar-c:bar-D -> E_P`, with `bar-c([g],[f])=[c0(g,f)]` and `bar-D=(q_P x q_P)(D0)`, exists iff (i) `D0` is saturated under `~P x ~P`, and (ii) equivalent defined input pairs have `~P`-equivalent outputs. This is the binary partial-map instance of T0.2/T0.4. Necessity pulls back the descended domain and value square; sufficiency is representative independence and surjectivity gives uniqueness.

**COR CP2.** The relation “`g` is composable after `f`” is L2-defined iff condition (i) holds. Otherwise it is `NOT DEFINED AT THIS QUOTIENT`, even when a raw composite happens to exist.

**THM CP3 (conditional associativity).** If CP1 holds and raw composition is equal, or merely quotient-equal, on every raw triple where both bracketings are defined, then `bar-c` is associative wherever both quotient bracketings are defined. It does not imply equality of bracketing domains, totality, identities, or category structure.

**PROP CP4 (typing and identity firewall).** Source/target maps descend by their own saturated-domain and fibre-constancy tests. A raw interface-indexed neutral assignment is an L2 identity family only if its index and assignment descend and both quotient identity laws hold on every required hom-family.

**NO-GO CP5.** Failure of CP1, type descent, or identity descent is not repaired by selecting an implementation: the relevant datum is `NOT DEFINED AT THIS QUOTIENT`.

## Second operation, transport, and prediction

**DEF CP9.** A second operation `boxdot:D_boxdot -> P0` is independent L1 input and passes CP1 independently. It is not a tensor; associativity, units, symmetry, and interchange are independent L2 claims.
