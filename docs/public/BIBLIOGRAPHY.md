# Bibliography and attribution

These are primary references and comparisons. The mathematical arguments used here are written out in the repository; obtaining a cited book or paper is not required to check them. No external paper, dataset or image is redistributed. Bibliographic identity is distinct from verification of an exact theorem attribution.

|---|---|
