# SS, modality and possible worlds

**Status: philosophical interpretation conditional on Structural Sufficiency (SS), using existing mathematics.** SS says more than that possible worlds have not been proved. Under SS, modal determinacy cannot vary independently of substantive differences and relations. Possibility, necessity and counterfactual dependence therefore have no additional ontological remainder over that organization. This is a consequence of adopting SS, not a mathematical proof of SS or a refutation of every competing metaphysics.

## What gives modality positive content?

Under SS, modality is a derivative articulation of actual relations and constraints, not an independent ontological layer. "Derivative" names explanatory dependence, not necessarily temporal succession. SS does not owe a derivation of modality from a wholly nonmodal basis: modal articulations express substantive constraints of actuality. Requiring a separate source of modality would reintroduce the modal primitive this position rejects.

A device can have an output it has not yet produced. Evidence for that capacity may come from its construction, couplings and response rules, not merely from uncertainty about its current output. A theory articulates such relations when it says what is compatible with specified conditions, what those conditions exclude, and how a specified change would affect an outcome. This content need not wait for human articulation to obtain.

Consider a supplied circuit model in which two readout bits must agree. Without another condition, its admitted pairs are 00 and 11. Given a first-bit record of 1, the second bit must be 1 within that model. Given 0 instead, it must be 0. These are definite conditional consequences. The two tuples are comparison cases for this task, not two independently existing worlds. Whether the circuit implements the relation requires evidence beyond the table.

Likewise, two devices can show the same idle readout while having different responses to a supported input. Equal idle records do not establish equal capacities. If a response relation is not supplied or justified, present ignorance does not settle whether the devices differ. Nor does it prove that any difference is inaccessible in principle, or that someone will eventually articulate it.

For a declared nonempty comparison class, a proposition is compatible when it has a witness and necessary relative to that class when every admitted case satisfies it. These are scoped theoretical meanings. An inconsistent class supplies no usable modal verdict; a missing specification supplies no verdict at all. Mathematical admissibility alone establishes neither an independently existing alternative nor a capacity of the described arrangement. Better constraints can correct an earlier modal attribution.

## Articulation and what it describes

A written sentence, a simulation run or an instrument record is itself produced in relations. That occurrence does not transfer its represented properties to what it describes. A simulation of a locked door opening supplies an implemented simulation; evidence that the door can open is a separate matter. Conversely, an incomplete simulation is not evidence that the omitted hinge makes no difference.

Here an articulation's occurrence concerns the specified production of that record or calculation. It introduces no distinguished global world or primitive marker of actuality. A human reading is one form of articulation, not a condition of substantive difference. SS neither requires exhaustive articulation nor promises universal comparability, eventual discovery or an evaluator with access to every truth.

The mathematical Q-test asks whether a particular attribution follows from retained content: does it remain invariant when forgotten details vary? A failure establishes insufficient content in that account. The philosophical SS claim is different: if a modal difference obtains, it must be a substantive organizational difference, rather than an extra fact stipulated to float free of organization. No particular account is thereby certified complete.

## Counterfactuals without a completed world catalogue

The circuit example supports a conditional because its response relation was supplied. Simply restricting records to cases with first bit 0 is conditioning, not a physical intervention. A claim about forcing that input requires a justified process or intervention rule and a statement of what it preserves. Different rules can yield different answers. SS supplies no universal similarity ranking, nearest-world selector or inference from observed correlation to causal dependence.

Concrete possibilities need not jointly assemble into one total history. Pairwise compatible constraints can fail jointly. No articulation, merely by occurring, establishes that it exhausts reality or closes what can be articulated. No catalogue of all possibilities is postulated.

## Connections and differences

These are limited philosophical comparisons, not reductions of the cited approaches to SS. Detailed audits and source-access limits are in the [research report](../../research/SS_MODALITY.md).

| Approach | Genuine connection and difference |
|---|---|
| Possible-worlds semantics | Evaluating claims over cases makes modal consequences explicit. A semantic model does not itself assert that its indices are independently existing worlds. SS uses declared comparison cases without adopting a complete world domain or a universal accessibility relation. See [Kripke, Semantical Considerations on Modal Logic](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-0346-0145-0_16). |
| Lewis's modal realism | It offers substantive modal content through an ontology of worlds, rather than mere ignorance. SS here grounds modal content in organization without adopting that ontology. Q diagnoses additional premises; it does not prove Lewis inconsistent. Worlds described structurally are not excluded merely by being called worlds. See [Lewis, On the Plurality of Worlds, selection](https://doi.org/10.7551/mitpress/5307.003.0007). |
| Modal fictionalism | World talk can be used without commitment to its literal world ontology. This reading of SS does not require an "according to the fiction" operator: justified capacities and dependencies can concern the described organization itself. See [Rosen, Modal Fictionalism](https://doi.org/10.1093/mind/XCIX.395.327). |
| Dispositional theories | Capacities can sustain counterfactual content without the relevant manifestation occurring. SS does not derive essential powers, property essences or a universal stimulus/manifestation analysis. See [Bird, Nature's Metaphysics, chapter 3](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199227013.003.0003). |
| Brandom's inferentialism | Modal vocabulary can make incompatibilities and counterfactual-supporting inferences explicit. Brandom also defends modal facts independent of concept-users; this is not a contrast between his alleged subjectivism and SS. The project adopts no discursive scorekeeper, social practice or inferentialist semantics as a foundation. See [Modal Expressivism and Modal Realism](https://sites.pitt.edu/~rbrandom/Texts/Modal%20Expressivism%20and%20Modal%20Realism%20HUP.docx). |
| Fine's truthmaker semantics | Partial states and exact verification give a finer account of content than truth across complete worlds alone. SS shares the interest in specific supporting relations, but Q-descent is not exact verification or a grounding theory. No Finean state space, fusion operation or verification relation is assumed. See [Fine, Truthmaker Semantics](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781118972090.ch22). |

## Open histories, zombies and limits

[Open Histories](OPEN_HISTORIES.md) distinguishes absolute historical uniqueness from a privileged history. A compatible alternative does not become another instantiated history merely because a theory represents it. Withholding absolute uniqueness still requires neither proof of many worlds nor branch probabilities; asserting multiplicity still requires substantive grounds.

The strengthened zombie contrast used here holds substantive organization fixed and alleges phenomenal variation; SS excludes that remainder within the position. The standard argument instead fixes physical facts, whose exhaustiveness is disputed, as explained in [Scope](SCOPE.md). The present modal interpretation does not prove that conceivability entails possibility, refute the opponent's modal argument, or equate equal reports with equal experience. See [the philosophical baseline](OVERVIEW.md) and [objections](SCOPE.md).

The open question concerns the adequacy of particular articulations: what warrants their constraints, capacities and counterfactual dependencies? The independent origin of a modal layer is not an additional unresolved task under SS. A genuine circle arises if "substantive organization" is defined solely through the modal conclusions it is then invoked to justify. Specifying response relations, conditions and their grounds must do more than restate the desired answer; these grounds need not be wholly nonmodal. This is a requirement on a particular explanation, not a demand for a separate ontological source of modality. SS's rejection of an independent modal remainder remains conditional on adopting SS; no current articulation is certified complete. The contribution is a consistent positive interpretation and explicit limits, not new mathematics, new physics or a universal theory of modality.
