# Argument map

The [baseline](OVERVIEW.md) is the canonical philosophical position. Critical objections are collected once in [Scope and limitations](SCOPE.md).

| Premise or input | Contribution | What does not follow |
|---|---|---|
| Experience as primary access | Retains felt determinacy as a starting point and explanandum | Infallibility or SS |
| Declared content and Q-exclusion | Prevents a fact requiring extra content from being presented as an old-theory consequence | Ontological nonexistence, identity of incomparable contents, or SS |
| SS | Rejects additional ontological determinacy beyond substantive organization; excludes the full zombie within the position | Global access, universal comparability, completed articulation, or adequacy of current neuroscience |
| Supplied operational interfaces | Specify local discrimination and usable access | Consciousness or a privileged system boundary |
| Sufficient records plus available decoders and stable updates | Explain conditional retention, reuse and control | Maintenance or construction merely from a static factor |
| Independently formed constraints and usable comparison | Can jointly determine a target before a separate verification | Absolute independence or global extension from local consistency |
| Construction and maintenance tables | Explain changes in enabled access and their ablation outcomes | Origin of previously unrepresented physical operations |

These are compatible commitments and conditional results. They are not a deduction from experience to a complete physics. SS asks whether a further ontological commitment is justified; Q-exclusion asks what follows from a given account. Conflating them would turn a relative mathematical test into an unsupported metaphysical proof.

## Ablation

| Remove | What survives | What is lost |
|---|---|---|
| SS | Experiential primacy and every mathematical result | Rejection of an additional phenomenal remainder; Q alone leaves enriched metaphysics open |
| The Q admission rule | The same tables and ordinary mathematical statements | Explicit policing of inference from retained content; standard sound reasoning can supply the same discipline |
| The label “Q-exclusion” alone | The admission rule and all mathematics | No independent mathematical content |
| Construction/record/resource dependencies | SS, static factors and constraint intersection | Predictions about which later reads remain enabled under construction, fuel and routing ablations |
| The label “endogenous articulation” alone | All specified mechanisms and predictions | No extra force or independent theorem |
| The word “perspective” | The operational specification | No technical result; its role is a carefully limited interpretive summary |

The explanatory synthesis connects determination, access, retention, update and maintenance while keeping their failure cases separate. It prevents a sufficient record from being mistaken for a working controller, or a local interface for consciousness. A comparative claim of improvement over existing scientific practice has not been tested.

## Novelty and contribution

| Kind | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Mathematics | Image factorization, quotient congruence, sufficient records, relational joins and finite transition arguments are known mathematics or scoped applications. No theorem-level novelty is certified. |
| Philosophy | SS and experiential primacy are adopted commitments with substantive opposing arguments. Compatibility checks do not confirm SS. |
| Explanatory synthesis | One auditable connection between scoped determination, implemented access and maintenance; no additional fundamental entity. |
| Open work | Justification of SS, experiential/scientific comparisons, implementation beyond supplied grammars and broader proof mechanization remain open. |
