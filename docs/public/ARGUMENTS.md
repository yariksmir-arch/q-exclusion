# Argument map

The [baseline](OVERVIEW.md) adopts SS while keeping its justification distinct from what a particular theory determines. The map below shows what each commitment contributes.

| Premise or input | Contribution | What does not follow |
|---|---|---|
| Experience as primary access | Retains felt determinacy as a starting point and explanandum | Infallibility or SS |
| Declared content and Q-exclusion | Prevents a fact requiring extra content from being presented as an old-theory consequence | Ontological nonexistence, identity of incomparable contents, or SS |
| SS | Rejects additional ontological determinacy beyond substantive organization; excludes the full zombie within the position | Global access, universal comparability, completed articulation, or adequacy of current neuroscience |
| Supplied operational interfaces | Specify local discrimination and usable access | Consciousness or a privileged system boundary |
| Sufficient records plus available decoders and stable updates | Explain conditional retention, reuse and control | Maintenance or construction merely from a static factor |
| Independently formed constraints and usable comparison | Can jointly determine a target before a separate verification | Absolute independence or global extension from local consistency |
| Construction and maintenance tables | Explain changes in enabled access and their ablation outcomes | Origin of previously unrepresented physical operations |

These are compatible commitments and conditional results. They are not a deduction from experience to a complete physics. SS rejects an independent ontological remainder; the philosophical dispute concerns the justification of that rejection. Q-exclusion asks what follows from a given account. Conflating them would turn a relative mathematical test into an unsupported metaphysical proof.

## Which claims depend on which inputs?

| Remove | What survives | What is lost |
|---|---|---|
| SS | Experiential primacy and every mathematical result | Exclusion of an independent ontological remainder; Q alone leaves enriched metaphysics open |
| Implemented probes, comparisons and decoders | Mathematical determination relative to supplied maps | A procedure for obtaining or using the answer |
| Construction, record and resource dependencies | SS, static factors and constraint intersection | Predictions about enabled access under construction, fuel and routing changes |

The explanatory synthesis connects determination, access, retention, update and maintenance while keeping their failure cases separate. A sufficient record need not be a working controller, and a local operational interface does not define consciousness. Comparative improvement over existing scientific practice has not been tested.

## Novelty and contribution

| Kind | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Mathematics | Image factorization, quotient congruence, sufficient records, relational joins and finite transition arguments are known mathematics or scoped applications. No theorem-level novelty is certified. |
| Philosophy | SS and experiential primacy are adopted commitments with substantive opposing arguments. Compatibility checks do not confirm SS. |
| Explanatory synthesis | One auditable connection between scoped determination, implemented access and maintenance; no additional fundamental entity. |
| Open work | Arguments for or against SS, adequacy of experiential/scientific comparisons, implementation beyond supplied grammars and broader proof mechanization remain open. Fixed-organization phenomenal variation and an independent modal remainder are excluded conditionally by SS; they are not unresolved internal requirements. |

## Historical uniqueness

SS does not entail absolute historical uniqueness. This claim requires its own structural grounds; withholding it requires neither proof of many worlds nor branch probabilities. A privileged history, distinguished from others by a criterion or selector, is a separate claim. Non-entailment of absolute historical uniqueness is distinct from positive evidence for multiple organizations and from a many-worlds ontology; positive multiplicity claims also require substantive grounds. See [open histories](OPEN_HISTORIES.md).

## Modal determinacy

Adopting SS excludes an additional modal fact stipulated to vary with substantive organization held fixed. This is a philosophical consequence of SS, not a theorem of Q. The Q-test instead asks which particular attributions descend from a stated account; an incomplete account can omit substantive differences. Compatibility in a model alone establishes neither an instantiated history nor a capacity of the described arrangement. See [the positive modal interpretation](MODALITY.md).
