# Scope and limitations

## What has been established

The published mathematics gives conditional determination, quotient, refinement, transport, record, control and constraint criteria. It uses known mathematics. The finite tests check witnesses and bounded enumerations; general proofs are textual, not proof-assistant verified. The philosophical position is consistent with those results. This does not confirm SS.

## How to read the objections

**VALID CHALLENGE** contests SS itself or a substantive inference. **CONDITIONAL RESOLUTION** follows from adopting SS and leaves the rival metaphysics disputed. **SEMANTIC SMUGGLING** imports a rejected primitive as an internal requirement. **MODEL ADEQUACY** concerns the warrant of a particular articulation. **CLOSED / KNOWN** marks an already established result, not a new research task. An external argument for a rejected primitive can still be a valid challenge to SS; its rejection alone does not refute that argument.

## Strong objections

**Zombies and conceivability — VALID CHALLENGE / CONDITIONAL RESOLUTION.** The standard argument holds the physical facts fixed and uses ideal conceivability to support a phenomenal difference. SS does not identify a complete physical description with exhaustive substantive determinacy. If the proposed duplicate holds substantive organization fixed as well, SS excludes phenomenal variation: this conditional consequence is settled within the position. The opponent can contest that commitment and the reach of structural explanation. Calling the physical duplicate incomplete without grounds would beg the question; Q-exclusion cannot settle the conceivability-to-possibility inference. See [Chalmers, §§3–5](https://consc.net/papers/nature.html).

**Mary and first-person knowledge — VALID CHALLENGE / CONDITIONAL RESOLUTION.** In Jackson's case Mary gains knowledge on seeing colour despite her supposed physical knowledge. SS can recognize a substantive change in access and acquaintance without accepting a new kind of ontological fact. The opponent argues that the gained knowledge is propositional and cannot be exhausted by that change. Calling it “just access” without addressing those grounds is insufficient; no factorization settles the issue. See [Jackson (1982), pp. 130–131](https://doi.org/10.2307/2960077).

**Explanatory gap — VALID CHALLENGE.** Explaining discrimination, control and report can leave phenomenal character unintelligible. SS wagers that this explanatory gap does not require an extra ontological remainder; it supplies no deduction of felt character from a behaviour table. The critic can challenge whether ontological economy adequately addresses that gap. Experience remains a starting access throughout. See [Chalmers (1995)](https://consc.net/papers/facing.pdf).

**Russellian monism and quiddities — VALID CHALLENGE / CONDITIONAL RESOLUTION.** A categorical basis may be proposed to explain why relational/dispositional descriptions leave phenomenal character unexplained. A basis allowed to vary while organization is held fixed is an added ontological commitment. Q diagnoses the extra content relative to that reduct, not its incoherence. A proposal identifying experiential character with the basis without an independently varying remainder needs separate assessment; the label “Russellian” alone does not decide its compatibility with SS. See [Chalmers, §11](https://consc.net/papers/nature.html).

**Thin structure and Newman’s objection — CLOSED / KNOWN limit; MODEL ADEQUACY.** Bare cardinality, an arbitrary extensional relation table and isomorphism do not establish substantive content. A substantive relation must be specified through its constraints, operations or interactions and the grounds for the attribution; merely naming it does not suffice. Semantic articulation and checking connect such claims to their subject matter through further relations, not an arbitrary assignment of physical meaning to mathematical labels or a selector of the correct referent. Transport preserves specified content; it cannot establish its adequacy. The live challenge is whether a chosen account supplies and justifies these relations without circularity. Demanding an independent nonstructural ground for every relation is **SEMANTIC SMUGGLING** as an internal requirement of SS, though a defended argument for that ground challenges SS externally. Adding substantively justified relations extends articulation; it does not establish a nonstructural residue. No absolute external criterion of content or solution to every version of Newman’s objection is claimed. See [Newman (1928)](https://doi.org/10.1093/mind/XXXVII.146.173).

**Tautology and hidden totality — VALID CHALLENGE to circular formulations; CONDITIONAL RESOLUTION of the total-description demand.** Organization cannot be defined as whatever ensures phenomenal determination, and appending a phenomenal bit cannot prove SS. Conversely, SS need not exhibit a complete account independently of experience. Its substantive risk is its rejection of a further ontological fact: a cogent argument for such a fact would challenge the wager. No set of all structures, perspectives or future procedures is assumed. Relative completeness of a specified grammar is an operational claim only.

**Behaviour is insufficient evidence of phenomenal exhaustiveness — CLOSED / KNOWN; MODEL ADEQUACY for particular comparisons.** Equal reports can accompany richer differences, and an instrument's operational interface supplies no consciousness. A missing local test or unavailable comparison establishes neither absence of experience nor identity of the contents at issue. Incomparability alone establishes no substantive difference either. These constraints also prevent an opponent's experiential evidence from being dismissed merely as an inaccessible label. Scientific and experiential comparisons require their own substantive grounds.

## Operational and physical boundaries

Available probes, routing, supported domains, stable updates and maintenance are distinct assumptions. Static sufficiency supplies no procedure. Enabling a word in an existing full grammar adds no full-grammar distinction. New physical operations require implementation evidence beyond a new table.

Formation audits certify declared non-importation, not absolute causal or statistical independence. An empty compatibility class is inconsistency; local consistency does not guarantee global extension. Abstract isomorphisms select no privileged representative and supply no physical process.

Incidence determines neither a metric nor spacetime. Exact represented intersections/unions differ from relative order bounds. Quantum record examples assume quantum mechanics; they do not derive it. No reconstruction of consciousness, QM, GR, fundamental time, objective chance or a uniquely natural system boundary is claimed. External datasets and physical validation are unnecessary for, and absent from, the finite checking route.

## Historical organization

[Open historical multiplicity](OPEN_HISTORIES.md) is interpretive permission under SS. Neither an objective totality of worlds nor an absolute history count, boundary or probability is inferred. Independent agreement does not automatically reconstruct causes; stable records need not select a unique history. Relational order is not automatically time. No physical branching mechanism or new mathematical theorem is claimed.

## Modal articulation and its warrant

In the [modal interpretation](MODALITY.md), modality is a derivative articulation of actual relations and constraints. This is explanatory dependence, not necessarily temporal succession or derivation from a wholly nonmodal basis. Requiring a separate ontological source would reinstate the rejected modal primitive. The remaining **MODEL ADEQUACY** challenge concerns the warrant for particular constraints, capacities and counterfactual dependencies. The independent-source demand is **SEMANTIC SMUGGLING** within SS; exclusion of an independent modal remainder is a **CONDITIONAL RESOLUTION**. A genuine circle would define organization solely through the modal conclusions it is supposed to justify; an explanation must supply grounds beyond restating those conclusions, without requiring wholly nonmodal grounds. SS rejects a separately varying remainder; Q does not certify that a chosen account captures the relevant organization, and no articulation is declared complete. Articulation occurrence is not truth of its content, mathematical compatibility is not implementation, and conditioning is not intervention. The zombie commitment concerns fixed substantive organization, never equal reports or present inability to distinguish.
