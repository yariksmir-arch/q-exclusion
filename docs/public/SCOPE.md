# Scope and limitations

## What has been established

The published mathematics gives conditional determination, quotient, refinement, transport, record, control and constraint criteria. It uses known mathematics. The finite tests check witnesses and bounded enumerations; general proofs are textual, not proof-assistant verified. The philosophical position is consistent with those results. This does not confirm SS.

Some objections contest SS itself; others concern the adequacy of a particular model. A consequence settled conditionally under SS does not refute a competing metaphysics. Conversely, requiring an independent remainder as a premise of SS would import the very commitment the position rejects.

## Strong objections

**Zombies and conceivability.** The standard argument holds the physical facts fixed and uses ideal conceivability to support a phenomenal difference. SS does not identify a complete physical description with exhaustive substantive determinacy. If the proposed duplicate holds substantive organization fixed as well, SS excludes phenomenal variation: this conditional consequence is settled within the position. The opponent can contest that commitment and the reach of structural explanation. Calling the physical duplicate incomplete without grounds would beg the question; Q-exclusion cannot settle the conceivability-to-possibility inference. See [Chalmers, §§3–5](https://consc.net/papers/nature.html).

**Mary and first-person knowledge.** In Jackson's case Mary gains knowledge on seeing colour despite her supposed physical knowledge. SS can recognize a substantive change in access and acquaintance without accepting a new kind of ontological fact. The opponent argues that the gained knowledge is propositional and cannot be exhausted by that change. Calling it “just access” without addressing those grounds is insufficient; no factorization settles the issue. See [Jackson (1982), pp. 130–131](https://doi.org/10.2307/2960077).

**Explanatory gap.** Explaining discrimination, control and report can leave phenomenal character unintelligible. SS wagers that this explanatory gap does not require an extra ontological remainder; it supplies no deduction of felt character from a behaviour table. The critic can challenge whether ontological economy adequately addresses that gap. Experience remains a starting access throughout. See [Chalmers (1995)](https://consc.net/papers/facing.pdf).

**Russellian monism and quiddities.** A categorical basis may be proposed to explain why relational/dispositional descriptions leave phenomenal character unexplained. A basis allowed to vary while organization is held fixed is an added ontological commitment. Q diagnoses the extra content relative to that reduct, not its incoherence. A proposal identifying experiential character with the basis without an independently varying remainder needs separate assessment; the label “Russellian” alone does not decide its compatibility with SS. See [Chalmers, §11](https://consc.net/papers/nature.html).

**Thin structure and Newman’s objection.** Bare cardinality, an arbitrary extensional relation table and isomorphism do not establish substantive content. A relation needs specification through its constraints, operations or interactions and grounds for its attribution; naming it does not suffice. Semantic articulation and checking connect a claim to its subject matter through further relations. They are neither arbitrary assignments of physical meaning to mathematical labels nor selectors of a privileged referent. Transport preserves specified content but does not establish its adequacy. The live challenge is whether a chosen account justifies its relations without circularity. Under SS, adding justified relations extends articulation; it does not establish a nonstructural residue or require an independent nonstructural ground for every relation. A defended argument for such a ground challenges SS externally. See [Newman (1928)](https://doi.org/10.1093/mind/XXXVII.146.173) and [the worked example](EXAMPLES.md).

**Tautology and hidden totality.** Organization cannot be defined as whatever ensures phenomenal determination, and appending a phenomenal bit cannot prove SS. Conversely, SS need not exhibit a complete account independently of experience. Its substantive risk is its rejection of a further ontological fact: a cogent argument for such a fact would challenge the wager. No set of all structures, perspectives or future procedures is assumed. Relative completeness of a specified grammar is an operational claim only.

**Behaviour is insufficient evidence of phenomenal exhaustiveness.** Equal reports can accompany richer differences, and an instrument's operational interface supplies no consciousness. A missing local test or unavailable comparison establishes neither absence of experience nor identity of the contents at issue. Incomparability alone establishes no substantive difference either. These constraints also prevent an opponent's experiential evidence from being dismissed merely as an inaccessible label. Scientific and experiential comparisons require their own substantive grounds.

## Operational and physical boundaries

Available probes, routing, supported domains, stable updates and maintenance are distinct assumptions. Static sufficiency supplies no procedure. Enabling a word in an existing full grammar adds no full-grammar distinction. New physical operations require implementation evidence beyond a new table.

Formation audits certify declared non-importation, not absolute causal or statistical independence. An empty compatibility class is inconsistency; local consistency does not guarantee global extension. Abstract isomorphisms select no privileged representative and supply no physical process.

Incidence determines neither a metric nor spacetime. Exact represented intersections/unions differ from relative order bounds. Quantum record examples assume quantum mechanics; they do not derive it. No reconstruction of consciousness, QM, GR, fundamental time, objective chance or a uniquely natural system boundary is claimed. External datasets and physical validation are unnecessary for, and absent from, the finite checking route.

## Historical organization

[Open historical multiplicity](OPEN_HISTORIES.md) is interpretive permission under SS. Neither an objective totality of worlds nor an absolute history count, boundary or probability is inferred. Independent agreement does not automatically reconstruct causes; stable records need not select a unique history. Relational order is not automatically time. No physical branching mechanism or new mathematical theorem is claimed.

## Modal articulation and its warrant

In the [modal interpretation](MODALITY.md), modality articulates substantive relations and constraints. Its dependence is explanatory, not necessarily temporal or a derivation from wholly nonmodal grounds. Under SS, no independent modal remainder is left to explain. Particular capacities and counterfactual dependencies still need warrant: defining organization solely through the conclusion it is meant to justify would be circular. Q certifies neither that warrant nor completeness of the chosen account. An articulation's occurrence does not establish its content, mathematical compatibility does not establish implementation, and conditioning is not intervention.
