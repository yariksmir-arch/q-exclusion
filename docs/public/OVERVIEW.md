# Philosophical baseline

**Status: philosophical position and explanatory synthesis (HEUR).** This page states the adopted position. Formal proofs use the [declared core](../../normative/01_FROZEN_CORE.md).

## Experience and structural determinacy

Experience is primary access to determinacy: the project begins with differences given in experience. This does not make introspection infallible. Immediate experience and scientific description are different ways of access and articulation, not two initial ontological categories. Their comparison requires substantive investigation; experience is not reduced to behaviour, reports or an operational interface. Primacy establishes neither a separate substance or absolute bearer, nor privileged first-person authority, nor experience as the ontological cause of all other relations. It does not make all differences already given, accessible or articulated. Whether a difference obtains does not depend on human recognition, measurement or report.

**Structural Sufficiency (SS) is the philosophical wager that there is no additional ontological determinacy beyond substantive differences and relations.** It concerns colour, unpleasantness, capacities and other concrete differences without sorting relations into two initial ontological domains. Experiential primacy and SS are connected but logically distinct commitments; neither alone proves the other. Excluding variation at fixed substantive organization is one consequence of SS, not its complete definition: a necessarily connected nonrelational ground would also add the ontological determinacy SS rejects. These consequences are settled conditionally within SS; the justification of SS remains contestable.

The positive claim is that felt character, capacities and evaluative differences belong to substantive organization; explaining them need not leave a second ontological task after their differences and dependencies have been accounted for. This is stronger than ontological economy, methodological agnosticism and supervenience; it does not certify current science as sufficient. It adds no general property of phenomenality to be explained after each concrete difference.

The argument asks what substantive work a proposed additional fact does. If it introduces a previously omitted dependence, difference or constraint, it enriches the theory and requires investigation. If it is postulated as an additional ontological ground beyond those differences and relations, SS excludes it, whether the proposed connection is contingent or necessary. A concrete explanatory asymmetry can instead be a substantive relation requiring its own grounds; no absolute priority follows merely from that asymmetry. Ontological economy supports this distinction but does not settle the dispute. An opponent may defend an enriched ontology on explanatory grounds; Q alone establishes no superiority of SS. Failure to identify additional content in a current articulation does not establish its absence in general. Organization cannot mean whatever guarantees the desired answer: encoding that answer can force factorization without justifying its explanation or SS. Current physics, neurophysiology and behaviour are resources for inquiry, without being certified to exhaust experience.

SS concerns substantive determinacy, not completeness of a description. Incomplete scientific knowledge can leave substantive relations unknown; this is a reason to improve the account, not evidence by itself for an independent nonstructural fact. An unarticulated relational difference may exceed any current account. What SS rejects as a *principled inarticulable residue* is a purported determinate difference placed outside any substantive relational articulation, rather than a difference unavailable to a particular interface. This rejection implies neither eventual discovery nor a completed articulation, and requires no exhaustive nonexperiential account from which experience must be produced.

## Content and grounds

Substantive structural determinacy is not a syntactic form. A unary predicate can express substantive relations and constraints; a binary relation can merely disguise an arbitrary label. For a particular claim, the audit asks:

1. **Content:** what difference, relation or constraint is asserted?
2. **Grounds:** what warrants attributing that content to the subject matter rather than only to its formal representation?

These are questions addressed to a concrete articulation, not new axioms, definitions of existence or a universal checking algorithm. Observability, an independent channel, predicate arity, experimental reproducibility and invariance under arbitrary rewritings are not additional mandatory criteria of content. A primary felt difference need not first have an external measurement procedure. Particular scientific claims still require grounds appropriate to what they assert.

Keep distinct the differences given in experience; their articulations and scientific explanations; formal determination relative to a declared theory, checked by Q; and the universal philosophical commitment of SS. These distinctions describe roles in inquiry, not four ontological domains. Givenness, articulability, the content of an articulation, warranted comparison and explanation of the relevant dependencies also answer different questions. Bare extensional structure does not supply substantive content. This limitation does not itself require a nonstructural bearer or ground of relations. Explaining a particular relation calls for the relevant dependencies and grounds, not a prior complete, independent description of every relation. Local explanations can be corrected or extended without waiting for that total inventory.

## Why universal scope requires no global perspective

SS itself is an articulation within relations. Its universal scope expresses a commitment about determinacy; it does not report an exhaustive survey of relations or a standpoint from which every difference can be compared. The commitment applies to its own formulation too: that formulation gains no exemption from the distinction between an articulation and what it describes. Stating SS neither certifies its truth nor makes the statement a complete description of its subject matter. This is self-application of the adopted commitment, not a further axiom or proof of SS. Its defence must remain open to argument without claiming a global perspective.

## Four questions that must remain distinct

| Question | What warrants an answer |
|---|---|
| What does a particular formal theory determine? | Its declared content, domains, target contrasts and proofs |
| What distinctions are accessible here? | The available repertoire, usable readouts, records and comparison procedures |
| Why adopt SS? | The philosophical argument against a further ontological remainder; open to substantive criticism |
| What does an opponent add? | An account of the proposed additional determinacy and grounds for treating it as ontologically additional to substantive relations. |

Q-exclusion addresses the first question. For specified maps U:B⁺→B and F:B⁺→Y, F is determined by retained content exactly when it is constant on U-fibres; the induced factor is unique on im U. A failed test means new content relative to this theory is required. It does not establish the nonexistence of that content. The set-level argument neither proves SS nor supplies an executable discriminator.

This is not verificationism. A difference unavailable through one interface may be articulated through another or in a richer theory. Missing access establishes neither identity nor difference; both claims need grounds. A quotient equates inputs only relative to its declared tests. It supplies no ontological identity or comparison between otherwise incomparable contents. Philosophical arguments about knowledge or conceivability also deserve assessment on their merits.

## Local access and independent constraints

**DEFINITION (interpretive terminology).** A perspective is a locally specified operational organization of available discriminations, relations and ways of access. Its dependencies are the specified procedures, readouts, records and usable comparisons. It is a summary role, not a new core object. Technical work uses “operational interface,” “local access,” “discrimination profile” and “available repertoire.”

This role can be described for a measuring instrument. Its definition supplies no consciousness, subject, bearer of experience, psychological viewpoint or independently constituted physical system. The operational definition does not encompass immediate phenomenal access; this difference in articulation introduces no second ontological layer. No collection of all perspectives is assumed.

Internal and external are relative to selective organization of interactions. Specify ports, intervention domains and supporting record/resource routes first; then test which dependencies mediate the task. External determinacy is constrained through interactions that can correct expectations. A choice of interface cannot arbitrarily choose those outcomes. Independent formation and held-out checks matter: copied reports or an echoing port are insufficient. Jointly usable constraints may fix a target that neither fixes alone. This supports a positive reconstruction of scientific objectivity through concrete relations and constraints: grounds for correction, supported comparisons, stability under specified changes and independent formation do different work. Agreement alone establishes no objectivity; plurality of models does not replace that work. No absolute independence, universal calibration or completed objective description is required. A scientific picture can be warranted and correctible without becoming an absolute foundation of all determinacy.

## Articulation and its supporting organization

Endogenous articulation names conditional changes in available discrimination through represented construction, coupling, recording and maintenance. A [sensor model](../../research/ENDOGENOUS_MODELS.md) makes the positive explanation concrete: construction enables a readout; its record routes replenishment; replenishment preserves later access. Removing construction, the resource input or the route has different testable consequences. Transparent classical access can preserve the organization.

These are ordinary control mechanisms. If build;read was already in the original generated grammar, enabling it changes immediate access, not the discriminating power of that full grammar. An operation absent from the presentation requires additional implementation evidence. No final global repertoire is needed. Structural sufficiency, local access completeness and completion of articulation therefore remain distinct.

Experience, memory and report also have different access conditions. Equal reports can compress different discriminations; their equality establishes no equality or absence of experience. Physiological and experiential comparisons require evidence preserving the meaning of the named contrasts. Such work can improve an account without first declaring either mode of access an independent substance.

## Concepts, generalization and explanation

A concept of a relation is an articulation; the concrete relation it can express need not be produced by forming that concept. Articulations themselves occur within relations and sometimes participate in what they describe, as an executed instruction can. Production, execution, correction and intervention can justify a particular distinction between articulation and subject matter; there is no universal boundary between two domains.

Generalization can omit distinctions needed for a particular question while supporting new laws, connections between models or comparisons. Greater generality establishes neither greater substantive completeness nor ontological fundamentality. A shared concept alone establishes no common nature. A supported transfer or explanatory connection can nonetheless justify unification; SS mandates neither model pluralism nor a single final theory.

Concept formation is not universally factorization of previously complete content. Learning, building procedures, comparing articulations, memory and changed practices can alter subsequent discrimination and correct earlier descriptions. These changes do not create every relation, cancel concrete explanatory asymmetries or establish absolute scientific priority.

Permissible loss for a stated task, underdetermination by a description, a changed question, acquisition of operations and substantive theory extension must be distinguished from explanatory circularity. Formal Q-failure requires a specified target varying on a forgetful fibre; an explanation can be circular despite successful factorization. SS's philosophical exclusion is a further, distinct commitment. None of these distinctions supplies a universal theory of conceptualization.

## Open historical multiplicity

The organization described by SS includes substantive transformations and their constraints, without requiring a completed description. Mathematical necessity can restrict how independently justified physical relations may combine: a boundary flux must enter a conservation balance, for example. It establishes consequences of the supplied conditions, not their physical realization or a unique physical theory. Extending a dynamical context preserves or refines old predictions only where preparations, operations and outcomes have justified comparison maps and the extended process respects them. A new influence or a corrected approximation can change those predictions. Openness neither guarantees preservation of every local formula nor rules out universal laws supported by independently justified constraints.

Adopting SS does not supply an absolutely unique history. A claim of absolute historical uniqueness needs structural grounds; withholding it requires neither many worlds nor branch probabilities. Historical distinctions acquire content through specified relations, records and articulation, without mandatory human recognition or absolute boundaries. Time and causal history require independently justified reconstruction from relations. See [the argument and its limits](OPEN_HISTORIES.md).

## Modality

Under SS, modality is a derivative articulation of actual relations and constraints; its dependence is explanatory, not necessarily temporal or a derivation from a wholly nonmodal basis. Modal determinacy has no additional ontological ground beyond those relations. Particular theories can articulate compatibility, exclusion, capacities and counterfactual dependence without certifying their own completeness. The occurrence of an articulation supplies no automatic warrant for its described content; missing access establishes no absence of substantive difference. See [SS, modality and possible worlds](MODALITY.md) for examples, objections and comparisons.

For biological function, moral reasons and responsible agency, [the normative application](NORMATIVITY.md) preserves substantive questions about suffering, justification and control while excluding an independent remainder conditionally under SS. Rejecting that remainder denies neither the phenomena nor the need to explain and assess them.
