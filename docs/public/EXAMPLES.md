# Worked examples

## One question followed through: what makes a capacity substantive?

### The philosophical problem

Two devices share an abstract two-position diagram and the same idle report. Does that establish the same capacity to respond to an input? If abstract structure leaves the answer open, must the missing content be nonstructural? This is the issue behind the project's use of Newman's objection: a bare pattern does not establish the substantive relations it purports to describe.

### The additional distinction

There are two different proposals. In the first, the devices have different supported response relations, omitted by the idle description. Their capacity difference has relational content. In the second, a further capacity difference is stipulated even with substantive organization held fixed. An account must not pass silently from the first proposal to the second. Matching idle reports, or even a supplied response table, does not establish exhaustive substantive identity.

### The Q-audit

Use the existing two-extension diagnostic from [the modal report](../../research/SS_MODALITY.md). Let U:{e₀,e₁}→{b} retain the same declared account, with F:{e₀,e₁}→{0,1} assigning different answers. These are chosen mathematical (L0) diagnostic carriers and maps, not devices or worlds; their equality contrasts belong to this stated test. Since U(e₀)=U(e₁) but F(e₀)≠F(e₁), the answer does not follow from that retained account. This is the familiar fibre criterion, not a new result.

The conclusion is **new content required relative to this account**, not that capacities are nonexistent or nonstructural. If U forgot a response relation, supplying and justifying it enriches the articulation. Arbitrarily attaching a physical meaning to the labels or choosing one extension supplies no such justification.

### What adopting SS adds

Under SS, a capacity difference must be a substantive organizational difference. The second proposal's independent modal remainder is excluded within that position, rather than left as an unresolved demand for a separate source of modality. Q alone does not establish this exclusion: it checks determination by the stated account, while SS is the additional philosophical commitment. Neither licenses treating the idle description as complete.

### The substantive question that remains

Which inputs are supported, what responses and dependencies obtain, and what grounds the model's attribution of them? Construction, coupling and justified intervention rules can answer parts of that question. A diagram alone cannot. Newman's warning against empty structure remains; it does not become an internal demand that every substantive relation rest on a nonstructural foundation. An opponent can still challenge SS or argue that the proposed relational account is circular. Those arguments concern its justification, not a consequence already proved by the Q-test.

The same separation matters for experience: equal reports do not establish equal phenomenal character, whereas a phenomenal difference stipulated at fixed substantive organization is excluded under SS. The [Mary and zombie objections](SCOPE.md) challenge that philosophical position; the diagnostic above does not model or explain consciousness.

## Finite illustrations

These existing witnesses isolate determination, access and retention. Their tables supply no proof of SS.

### A report can omit a distinction

Let X={00,01,10,11}. The readout a returns the first bit and the target b the second. Since a(00)=a(01) but b(00)≠b(01), no decoder of a determines b. Any relabelling or classifier d(a) retains this failure. An implemented b probe can change access. A mathematical function named b supplies no instrument by itself. This is the core factorization and available-refinement countermodel.

### Static sufficiency can fail under update

A record on {a,b,c} returns 0,0,1. It suffices for that present readout. An update taking a to a and b to c sends equal records to unequal records; no deterministic record-only update descends. An operation enabled at a and disabled at b fails for a different reason: its domain is not saturated. The [control proofs](../../research/CONTROL_THEORY.md) and finite control suites distinguish both failures.

### Independent constraints can correct an answer

Two independently formed restrictions retain {1,2} and {1,3}. A usable common-target comparison restricts their nonempty joint class to {1}. Neither restriction alone determines it. If the comparison is absent, simply intersecting labels assumes missing content; if one report copied the other, independence of formation has not been shown. These are ordinary constraint and provenance conditions, not arbitrary choice by an interface.

Local agreement need not extend globally. Binary constraints a=b, b=c and c≠a each have nonempty pair tables and matching one-port projections, yet their conjunction has no assignment. The result is inconsistency of that finite task, not selection of a global history. See [joint constraints](../../research/INDEPENDENT_CONSTRAINT_CONVERGENCE.md) and [coordination](../../research/COORDINATION.md).

### Construction can enable and maintain a read

In the [sensor model](../../research/ENDOGENOUS_MODELS.md), build consumes a supplied resource and enables a sensor. Reading records x; the recorded value selects the replenishment route; decay then returns the fuel to its original level. The invariant follows by finite induction. Removing replenishment exhausts fuel; choosing a fixed wrong route fails on one preparation. A zero-cost complete peek preserves the loop, refuting necessary informational hiddenness.

The full original grammar already contains build;read, so it already distinguishes x. Immediate access changes; no new full-grammar contrast is created. Previously unrepresented physical operations need additional implementation evidence.

### Reports and experience are different access conditions

Consider declared contrasts E={a,b,c,d}, memory m mapping a,b to 0 and c,d to 1, and a report mapping both memory values to one label. The report loses distinctions retained in E and m. This conditional diagram concerns compression. Reading E as experiential contrasts requires its own evidence; it is not a definition or derivation of consciousness. Equal reports establish no equality or absence of experience. See [the typed compression example](../../research/INTERFACE_QUESTIONS.md).
