# Project Charter

## Objective
Build a developed logical-mathematical theory around the Q-free core, with special emphasis on prediction, refinement, formal invariance, model families, and late recognition of physical mathematics.

## Primary deliverables
1. A minimal axiomatic/metatheoretic core.
2. Definitions and universal properties of contextual empirical quotients.
3. Descent and refinement theorems.
4. Presentation-invariance/metaequivalence theorems.
5. A systematic model zoo and countermodel suite.
6. A formal predictive layer: finite records, compatible profiles, stability, set-valued prediction, refinement compatibility.
7. A modular recognition layer for probability, measurement, geometry, operator theory, fields, symmetries and gauge — without promoting them to the core.
8. A broad regression proving that the theory remains Q-free and non-circular after expansion.

## Explicit non-goals
- Do not prove that science, observers, or our universe are necessary.
- Do not derive complex QM by axiom choice.
- Do not derive GR or the Standard Model by importing their structures.
- Do not posit a final empirical quotient or final global state.
- Do not claim physical uniqueness where only representation uniqueness within a stated mathematical class has been shown.

## Success criteria
The program is successful if it yields:
- a small stable backbone;
- formal metatheorems that survive presentation changes;
- nontrivial examples and counterexamples computed in one uniform framework;
- at least one original emergence theorem beyond mere renaming of known reconstruction results;
- explicit no-go boundaries for what the core cannot determine;
- a recognition layer cleanly separated from emergence.

## Preferred theorem style
Prefer statements of the form:

`weak structural conditions -> precise quotient/descent/refinement property`

or

`empirical signature with explicit properties -> representation class`

Avoid:

`desired physical theory -> rewrite its axioms in structural language -> call it emergence`.
