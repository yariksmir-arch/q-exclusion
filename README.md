# Q-Exclusion

The project begins with differences given in experience. **Experience is primary access to determinacy.** Colour, pain, remembered change and experienced action supply concrete starting differences; their articulation and explanation can be corrected. Experience is no separate substance, privileged first person or ontological cause of all relations. Scientific descriptions arise within inquiry and can transform subsequent discrimination; their success does not automatically make them an absolute foundation of the determinacy through which they are constructed and checked.

**Structural Sufficiency (SS)** is the related, logically distinct commitment that there is no additional ontological determinacy beyond substantive differences and relations. This excludes an additional nonrelational ground even if its connection to those relations is claimed to be necessary; it is stronger than supervenience or a ban on freely varying parameters. SS requires neither a complete description nor a global perspective. It institutes no initial phenomenal/nonphenomenal division and does not say that every relation is phenomenal.

The philosophical zombie is a useful [illustration](docs/public/EXAMPLES.md), without adopting its physical/phenomenal division as the project's starting ontology. Equal behaviour or a physical description does not establish exhaustive substantive identity. A demand for an extra fact after a concrete difference has been explained must specify what it adds.

**Q-exclusion** separates two tasks. The mathematical Q-test checks whether a specified answer follows from a stated account; failure calls for new content relative to that account. SS separately excludes an independent ontological remainder. When an allegedly inexplicable residue appears, first examine whether the question itself introduced that extra requirement. Missing knowledge alone establishes no such remainder, and the Q-test does not prove SS.

The positive result is a more precise division of explanatory work: which particular differences and dependencies need accounting for, what supports a capacity or counterfactual, and which circumstances matter to responsibility? These questions retain their content under SS. The [worked examples](docs/public/EXAMPLES.md) distinguish them from the extra ontological demand. Known factorization, quotient, record and control mathematics checks what follows from supplied content and available access; no theorem-level mathematical novelty is claimed.

## Read

- **Philosopher, 15–20 minutes:** [worked examples](docs/public/EXAMPLES.md) → [baseline](docs/public/OVERVIEW.md) → [argument map](docs/public/ARGUMENTS.md) → [objections and limits](docs/public/SCOPE.md).
- **Mathematician, 30–60 minutes:** [formal guide and proof map](docs/public/RESULTS.md) → linked definitions, proofs and countermodels → [reproducibility](docs/public/REPRODUCIBILITY.md).

Applications, each with its own stated limits:

- [Structuralist consciousness research: comparisons and objections](docs/public/PHENOMENAL_STRUCTURALISM.md): comparisons with structuralist consciousness research and the Mary, novel and isolated experience objections; philosophical assessment with open adequacy questions.
- [Modality and possible worlds](docs/public/MODALITY.md): capacities, constraints and counterfactuals without a completed world catalogue; interpretation conditional on SS.
- [Open histories](docs/public/OPEN_HISTORIES.md): why SS does not entail absolute historical uniqueness; interpretive permission, not evidence for many worlds.
- [Normativity, ethics and free will](docs/public/NORMATIVITY.md): function, moral revision, control and responsibility; conditional application using existing theories, without deriving an ethical verdict.

The [comparison map](docs/public/ARGUMENTS.md#philosophical-neighbours) locates the philosophical wager among its predecessors.

## Verify

With Python 3.12 and Windows PowerShell, from this repository root:

~~~powershell
python -B research/regression/canonical_regression.py
python -B research/verification/open_histories_check.py
python -B research/verification/verify_public.py
~~~

No package installation, external dataset, account or service is required for local checks. See [reproducibility](docs/public/REPRODUCIBILITY.md) for additional model checks, clean reconstruction and CI. Finite checks are not proofs of general claims.
